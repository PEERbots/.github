# Peerbots Brand Voice & Messaging Guide

This is the canonical messaging and tone guide for **Peerbots, Inc.** across repositories, READMEs, documentation, trainings, and public updates.

---

## Core Tagline & Boilerplate

**Signature Tagline:**
> **Peerbots makes it easy to learn about, design, and control social robots that you build from materials you already have.**

**One-Sentence Mission:**
> **Peerbots, Inc.** is a 501(c)(3) research-informed tech nonprofit on a mission to build social robots for everyone, powered by experts.

**Platform Overview (Medium):**
> With the free Peerbots Platform, anyone can turn a phone, tablet, or screen into an expressive robot face, author robot interactions, share scripts in the Peerbots Marketplace, and control robots directly — including the Peerbots Face, Misty, and Furhat — without programming experience or vendor lock-in. We also design 3D-printable robot building blocks (Moddy) and offer free tutorials, trainings, technical support, and custom implementation services.

## Core Messaging Themes (Mix & Match by Audience)

1. **People-First, Not AI-First**:
   - Social robotics is full of systems deferring to AI for full robot control. An AI-only approach gives up on the experts — therapists, educators, parents, artists — who actually make interactions succeed, especially with children and vulnerable populations.
   - Peerbots builds **tools, not black-box automations**, putting experts and end users in full control to tell their own stories in their own way.
   - Where we incorporate AI responsibly, it supports the human rather than replacing them (for example, simulating a practice audience so therapists can rehearse and author their own responses).
2. **Raising the Floor, Not Just the Ceiling**:
   - Academic robotics often focuses on raising the ceiling of what is technically possible. Peerbots collaborates with the research community (such as HRI) to **raise the floor** — removing cost and technical barriers through accessible, low-cost, end-user-focused design.
3. **Universal Design & Neurodiversity-Affirming Co-Design**:
   - Peerbots started by helping therapists and special educators use social robots with autistic children — and by making tools that are easy for therapists and engaging for autistic kids, we make tools that work better for everyone.
   - We co-design with people with lived experience of disability as advisors and partners, pursuing accessibility, agency, and social justice through technology.
4. **No Profit Motive — Privacy & Agency First**:
   - Unlike profit-driven tech companies that extract user data or lock users in, Peerbots is a grant- and community-supported 501(c)(3) nonprofit. That gives us the freedom to build what people actually want without hidden agendas.
5. **Robot-Agnostic & Built From Materials You Already Have**:
   - Social robots are often pricey and locked to proprietary platforms. Peerbots turns any screen you already own into a robot face, works across multiple physical robots (Peerbots Face, Misty, Furhat), and lets you prototype 3D-printable hardware (Moddy) without lock-in.
6. **STEAM & the Arts**:
   - Social robotics is about far more than code — it brings together body design, character design, conversation design, storytelling, theatre, sewing, and crafting. Anyone can start with zero programming experience and grow into coding and APIs when ready.

## Who Uses Peerbots (Example Audiences)

- **Therapists & Special Educators**: Control a robot facilitator during sessions with children for engaging, safe, neurodiversity-affirming interactions.
- **Teachers, Librarians & Outreach Leaders**: Run accessible STEAM workshops and summer camps with no coding prerequisite.
- **Parents & Families**: Read a story or play with a child using a phone-based robot face while controlling exactly what the robot says.
- **Artists, Performers & Script Writers**: Direct interactive art installations or theatrical performances with full creative control.
- **Researchers**: Run repeatable human-robot interaction (HRI) studies across participants and multiple robot platforms.
- **Developers & Makers**: Integrate sensors, build custom experiences with our API and npm packages, or 3D-print custom Moddy robots.

## Tone & Writing Standards

- **Plain Language First (ISO 24495)**: Warm, concrete, approachable, and jargon-free.
- **User Capabilities Over Dev Jargon**: For public release notes and Canny updates, lead with what educators, therapists, artists, or makers can now do — not internal refactors or package bumps.
- **Terminology**: `Peerbots` (`Peerbots, Inc.` in legal notices), `Peerbots Platform` (`app.peerbots.org`), `Peerbots Marketplace` (`market.peerbots.org`), `Moddy`, `@peerbots/face`, `@peerbots/core`.
