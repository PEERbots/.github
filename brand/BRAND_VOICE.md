# Peerbots Brand Voice & Messaging

## Who We Are (Standard Boilerplate)

**Short (1 sentence):**
> **Peerbots, Inc.** is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit building free, open-source, research-informed social robotics tools designed to keep humans in control.

**Medium (2–3 sentences):**
> **Peerbots** empowers educators, therapists, parents, researchers, and makers to use social robots in their fields — no robotics background required. Our open-source ecosystem includes a web-based robot controller, expressive 2D and 3D robot faces, a community behavior marketplace, and 3D-printable modular hardware.

## Voice & Tone Principles

1. **Plain Language First (ISO 24495)**: Write for real people — teachers, clinicians, families, students, and developers alike. Avoid unnecessary robotics jargon; when technical terms are needed, explain them simply.
2. **Human-Centered & Empowering**: Always emphasize that **humans remain in control** of the robot. Peerbots is a tool that amplifies human connection and expertise, never a replacement for human care.
3. **Warm, Inclusive & Welcoming**: Assume readers come from diverse backgrounds, accessibility needs, and technical skill levels.
4. **Research-Informed, Never Pretentious**: Ground claims in evidence and real-world practice without sounding dry or inaccessible.
5. **User Capabilities Over Dev Jargon (for Canny / Public Updates)**: When writing public release notes, announcements, or Canny changelog entries, lead with *what educators, therapists, or makers can now do* — not internal refactors or dependency bumps.

## Terminology Standards

- **Organization**: `Peerbots` (or `Peerbots, Inc.` in legal/copyright notices)
- **Main Web App**: `Peerbots Platform` (`app.peerbots.org`)
- **Marketplace**: `Peerbots Marketplace` (`market.peerbots.org`)
- **3D-Printable Robot**: `Moddy` (not `Mod-dy` or `BlockaNoid` in user-facing copy)
- **Robot Face Package**: `@peerbots/face`
- **Design System Package**: `@peerbots/core`
