# Peerbots Support

Need help using Peerbots, setting up a robot face, printing Moddy, or contributing code? Here's where to go:

- **Questions, Q&A & Ideas**: Start a thread in [Peerbots GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/PEERbots/.github/discussions).
- **Free Self-Paced Trainings**: Explore our open training courses in [Peerbots Trainings (`course-in-a-box`)](https://github.com/PEERbots/course-in-a-box).
- **Bug Reports**: Open a bug report in the relevant repository's **Issues** tab (please avoid posting private emails or personal data in public issues).
- **Hands-On Training, Research Partnerships & Support**: Reach out via [peerbots.org/contact](https://www.peerbots.org/contact) or email [support@peerbots.org](mailto:support@peerbots.org).
- **Security Issues**: Follow [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) to report privately.
